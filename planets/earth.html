PLANET EARTH

Welcome to Earth, our home planet and the third planet from the Sun. With vast oceans, diverse landscapes, a protective atmosphere, and an extraordinary variety of life, Earth is the only world currently known to support life.

01 / PLANET PROFILE

Meet Earth

Our home is a dynamic rocky planet with liquid water on its surface, a life-supporting atmosphere, and a magnetic field.

Our Home in Space

Earth formed about 4.54 billion years ago. Its surface includes continents, oceans, mountains, deserts, forests, and polar regions. Water covers roughly 71% of its surface.


Earth's atmosphere helps regulate temperature and provides gases essential to many forms of life. Its magnetic field also helps protect the planet from charged particles from space.

02 / INSIDE THE PLANET

The Internal Structure of Earth

Earth has several layers, each with different properties and roles in the planet's evolution.

01

Inner Core

The inner core is a solid, extremely hot region composed mainly of iron and nickel. Enormous pressure keeps it solid despite its high temperature.

02

Outer Core

The outer core is a layer of liquid metal. Its movement helps generate Earth's magnetic field.

03

Mantle

The mantle is a thick layer of hot rock. Slow movement within it helps drive the motion of tectonic plates.

04

Crust

The crust is Earth's thin outer solid layer. It forms the continents and ocean floors and is divided into moving tectonic plates.

Did you know? Earth's magnetic field is generated largely by the movement of electrically conducting liquid metal in the outer core.
03 / WHAT IS EARTH MADE OF?

Composition of Earth

Earth contains solid rock, liquid water, a gaseous atmosphere, and a metallic interior.

🪨

Rock and Minerals

Earth's crust contains a wide variety of rocks and minerals that form mountains, plains, and the ocean floor.

🌊

Liquid Water

Oceans, rivers, lakes, glaciers, and groundwater form part of Earth's water system and support many living organisms.

🌬️

Atmosphere

Earth's atmosphere consists mainly of nitrogen and oxygen, along with smaller amounts of other gases.

04 / OUR PROTECTIVE SHIELD

Atmosphere and Climate

Earth's atmosphere and climate systems help create conditions in which life can thrive.

☁️

Atmospheric Layers

The atmosphere includes the troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere, thermosphere, and exosphere. Each layer has distinct characteristics.

🌡️

Climate Regulation

The atmosphere, oceans, land, and ice interact to distribute heat and influence weather and long-term climate.

🛡️

Natural Protection

The atmosphere blocks or absorbs much harmful solar radiation, while the magnetic field deflects many charged particles from the Sun.

Why is Earth habitable? Earth's liquid water, suitable temperatures, atmosphere, energy sources, and chemical building blocks support the life we know.
05 / A LIVING WORLD

Life on Earth

Earth is home to an extraordinary diversity of organisms living in many different environments.

Land Ecosystems

Forests, grasslands, deserts, and tundra support plants, animals, fungi, and countless microorganisms adapted to their surroundings.

Marine Ecosystems

Oceans contain habitats ranging from sunlit coral reefs to deep-sea environments. Marine organisms play important roles in global food webs and nutrient cycles.

Biodiversity

Biodiversity includes the variety of species, genes, and ecosystems. Protecting it helps maintain the resilience of natural systems.

Human Responsibility

Pollution, habitat destruction, climate change, and resource overuse can threaten ecosystems. Conservation helps protect Earth's living systems.

06 / OUR NATURAL SATELLITE

Earth and the Moon

Earth's Moon is a major feature of our planet's celestial neighborhood.

The Moon

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It orbits Earth and reflects sunlight, producing the familiar phases seen from our planet.


The Moon's gravitational pull is the main driver of Earth's ocean tides. It also helps stabilize changes in Earth's axial tilt over long periods.

07 / OUR PLACE IN THE UNIVERSE

Why Earth Matters

Earth is our home and the only known planet where life has been confirmed.

💧

Water for Life

Liquid water supports essential biological processes and shapes Earth's landscapes and climate.

🌱

Natural Resources

Earth's soil, water, minerals, forests, and energy resources support ecosystems and human societies.

🔭

Understanding Other Worlds

Studying Earth helps scientists compare planets and search for potentially habitable environments elsewhere.

08 / OBSERVING OUR PLANET

Earth from Space

Satellites and space missions help us observe our planet on a global scale.

Earth Observation Satellites

Satellites measure clouds, rainfall, ocean conditions, vegetation, ice cover, and many other features of Earth's changing environment.

International Space Station

The International Space Station provides a platform for scientific research in low Earth orbit and offers unique views of our planet.

Weather Satellites

Weather satellites help meteorologists track storms, monitor cloud systems, and improve forecasts.

Climate Monitoring

Space-based instruments track long-term changes in temperature, sea level, ice, and atmospheric gases.

09 / DID YOU KNOW?

Interesting Facts About Earth

Discover some fascinating features of our home planet.

🌊

The Blue Planet

Earth appears blue from space because much of its surface is covered by oceans.

🌙

One Natural Moon

Earth has one permanent natural satellite, which influences tides and illuminates the night sky by reflecting sunlight.

🧲

A Magnetic Field

Earth's magnetic field creates a protective region around the planet called the magnetosphere.

🌍

Moving Continents

Earth's tectonic plates move slowly, changing the positions of continents and ocean basins over millions of years.

💧

Abundant Surface Water

About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water, although most of it is salty ocean water.

☀️

Perfect for Life?

Earth has conditions suitable for the life we know, but scientists continue investigating how life began and whether it exists elsewhere.
